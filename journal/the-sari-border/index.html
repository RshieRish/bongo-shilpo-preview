    Reading a sari border

    Compare a full vermilion drape with the gold threads that finish its edge.

    Start with the line that runs from the shoulder across the body. It shows how the border changes direction when the pallu is draped. At the hem it becomes a narrower horizontal edge.

    The small scattered motifs leave the red ground visible, while the denser edge catches light. These photographs illustrate the demo textile and its drape.
